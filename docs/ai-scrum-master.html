What Is an AI Scrum Master?

A traditional Scrum Master manually tracks team progress, facilitates ceremonies, and identifies blockers during stand-ups and retrospectives. This works — but it's slow, manual, and requires constant attention.

An AI Scrum Master like Scrumb continuously analyzes your team's development activity in real time, surfacing risks and insights before they become problems. Instead of discovering a blocker in Friday's retrospective, you know about it on Tuesday.

What Does Scrumb's AI Scrum Master Do?

  • Assists with sprint planning using AI-generated story breakdowns and timelines
  • Detects development blockers and risks before they impact sprint delivery
  • Tracks individual and team progress automatically, without manual check-ins
  • Provides velocity insights to improve future sprint accuracy
  • Operates directly inside your JetBrains IDE — no tab switching

AI Scrum Master vs. Traditional Scrum Master

Traditional Scrum Master

  • Manual progress tracking
  • Blockers discovered in stand-ups
  • Relies on team self-reporting
  • Insights come after retrospectives
  • Separate from dev environment

Scrumb AI Scrum Master

Why Teams Use Scrumb

Most agile teams — especially student groups, early-stage startups, and small engineering teams — don't have a dedicated Scrum Master. Meetings get skipped, blockers pile up unnoticed, and sprints fail not because the team can't execute, but because no one had visibility.

Scrumb fills that gap. It's an AI co-pilot for your agile workflow, running in the background and surfacing the information your team needs to stay on track.

Who Should Use an AI Scrum Master?

  • Student teams running capstone projects or hackathons with agile methodology
  • Startups that can't afford or don't yet need a full-time Scrum Master
  • Engineering teams looking to reduce sprint failure rates
  • Developers who want to self-manage with structured agile tooling
