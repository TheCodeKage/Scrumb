AI Scrum Master for Agile Teams

Scrumb is an AI-powered Scrum Master designed to help agile teams plan better sprints, identify blockers early, and ensure projects actually get completed.

What Does an AI Scrum Master Do?

  • Assist with sprint planning
  • Detect blockers before they impact the sprint
  • Track team progress automatically
  • Provide insights to improve productivity

Why Teams Use Scrumb

Traditional Scrum Masters rely on manual tracking and meetings. Scrumb uses AI to continuously analyze development activity and provide real-time sprint insights.
