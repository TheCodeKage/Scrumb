Frequently Asked Questions

What is Scrumb?

Scrumb is an AI-powered Scrum Master designed to help agile teams plan sprints and detect blockers.

Is Scrumb available now?

Scrumb is currently in early access.

Does Scrumb integrate with JetBrains IDEs?

Yes, a JetBrains plugin is available in early access.

Who should use Scrumb?

Students, startups, and engineering teams working with agile workflows.
